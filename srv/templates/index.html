Welcome to Remember Everything

A todo and notes app with spaced repetition. Here’s how it works.

Review

The Review tab shows items that are due for spaced repetition using the SM-2 algorithm:

You answer with two options on each card:

  • Didn't remember: you couldn't recall the answer
  • Remembered: you successfully recalled the answer

Answer "Remembered" to keep the item in your long-term memory — its interval grows. Answer "Didn't remember" to reset it to the beginning. The more consistently you say "Remembered", the longer your review intervals become.

Todos

Quickly add todos. Check them off when done. Todos are also eligible for spaced repetition — making sure you remember to do things, not just list them.

Notes

Jot down knowledge you want to retain. Add cloze deletions (fill-in-the-blank prompts) to turn a note into multiple review cards.

Search

Find any item instantly. Searches both titles and content.

Cloze Deletions

A cloze deletion hides part of a note so you can test yourself on it. To make one:

  1. Open a note for editing.
  2. Select the text you want to hide.
  3. Click Make cloze or press Ctrl+Shift+C (Cmd+Shift+C on Mac).

For example, a note titled The capital of France is {{Paris}} becomes a review card that asks: “The capital of France is […]”

You can have multiple clozes in one note — each is reviewed independently. Only one cloze per note is shown per day so you don’t see the answer on the next card.

Tips

  • Items with clozes automatically use cloze-based spaced repetition instead of whole-item review.
  • You can disable reviews for any item in the edit modal.
  • Resetting reviews clears the history and starts the item from scratch.
  • Works offline thanks to the service worker.

Backup & Restore

All your data lives in this browser only. Export it to a file so you never lose it — including when moving to a new device, browser, or hosting URL. No login required.

Import always adds to your existing data — it never deletes or overwrites anything. A todo or note is skipped if its type and title exactly match one you already have, so your existing progress on that item is kept; anything new is added.
